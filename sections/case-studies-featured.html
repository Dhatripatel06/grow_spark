Rebuilding a service delivery model across nine offices

An illustrative engagement, representative of how we structure this class of transformation work.

Professional Services · Operating Model

A multi-office advisory firm was growing revenue but losing consistency

Client type

A nine-office professional services firm expanding into new regional markets.

Challenge

Client experience varied significantly by office. Each location had built its own delivery process, reporting cadence and pricing logic, which slowed onboarding and made performance difficult to compare across the business.

What Grow Spark did

We diagnosed the operating model office by office, designed a single delivery framework the whole firm could run on, and implemented shared systems for client onboarding, reporting and pricing — then worked with regional leads through rollout.
